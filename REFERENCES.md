**Checked** says how each entry was last confirmed:
- **Abstract:** the published abstract was re-read on 5 Nov 2026 (R37 onwards: 7 Nov 2026), and the "Supports" text matches it.
- **Part:** the abstract confirms some of the "Supports" text; the part marked *full text* still needs checking against the paper.
- **Not re-checked:** read when the card was written, but couldn't be reopened on 5 Nov 2026.
- **Page:** the page or handout was read on 6 Nov 2026 (R37 onwards: 7 Nov 2026).
- **Full:** the paper itself was read on 7 Nov 2026.
- **Summary:** the paper was blocked; only a published summary, abstract listing or search result was seen on 7 Nov 2026.
